Freedom Park Rules & Policies

To ensure the safety, cleanliness, and peace of all guests, please review our park guidelines.

General Park Guidelines

  • Operating Hours: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. All activities and checkouts must conclude by 8:00 PM.
  • Maximum Capacity: 100 guests per booking. Overcrowding beyond 100 persons is strictly prohibited.
  • Sole Booking Policy: The booking is for your private party only. Entry of uninvited individuals is restricted.
  • Sports & Play Equipment: Equipment provided during check-in must be returned in good condition during checkout. Any missing or damaged items will be documented.
  • Cleanliness & Environment: Please keep the park grounds clean. Waste disposal bins are placed throughout the premises.
  • Safety: Children should be supervised near sports areas.
