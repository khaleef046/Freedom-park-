About

Welcome to Freedom Park

A private sanctuary in Pernambut designed for family gatherings and celebrations.

The Freedom Park Story

Freedom Park was established with a singular vision: to give families in and around Pernambut a genuine, private haven to unwind, celebrate, and spend meaningful time together away from commercial distractions and public crowds.

Unlike conventional parks or public spaces where multiple groups overlap, Freedom Park operates on a strict One Day = One Booking model. When your family reserves a date, our gates are dedicated exclusively to you and your invited guests (up to 100 people) from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Our Core Principles

  • Total Privacy: Only your group has access for the entire 12-hour booking duration.
  • Natural Hospitality: Shaded trees, verdant lawns, and fresh air maintained with meticulous care.
  • Family Security: Professional on-site security check-in ensures only verified guests enter.
  • Honest Pricing: Flat day rate with zero hidden per-head surcharges.
Leadership & Vision

Founded by M. Asrar Ul Haq

Freedom Park was conceived, created, and founded by M. Asrar Ul Haq with the dedication to provide families in Pernambut and surrounding communities an exclusive, safe, and scenic green sanctuary to gather, celebrate, and create cherished memories together in total privacy.
